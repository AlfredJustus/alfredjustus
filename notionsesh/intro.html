NotionSesh Intro

From Web Content to Clean Notion Blocks in Minutes

NotionSesh helps you move text, screenshots, and extracted content into Notion with 1:1 block mapping so formatting stays consistent.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Open the sidepanel

Open NotionSesh from your extension toolbar and pin it if you use it often. Keep the panel open while collecting content.

2

Copy & paste content directly

Paste content from websites, LLM chats, docs, and notes straight into the editor. Shortcut: Ctrl + V (or ⌘ + V on Mac).

3

Use Smart Extract for hard-to-copy areas

Click Extract to select a region in PDFs, slides, or image-heavy pages. NotionSesh converts detected content into editable Notion-style blocks.

4

Use Screenshot when you want visuals preserved

Click Screenshot to capture a region as an image block. This is ideal when exact visual fidelity matters more than text extraction.

5

Review and adjust in the editor

Reorder blocks, edit text, and tidy structure before exporting. You can combine pasted, extracted, and screenshot content in one workflow.

6

Copy to Notion and paste

Click Copy to Notion, then paste into any Notion page. Blocks are transferred in a Notion-friendly structure for clean results.

Pro Tips

  • Use Extract for mixed text/math layouts and Screenshot for exact visual snippets.
  • Keep your panel open while collecting multiple sources into one final paste.
  • If your OS uses reduced motion settings, animations can be toned down automatically.
